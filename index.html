Uncertificated Securities Market (USM)

Hong Kong's Uncertificated Securities Market regime takes effect on 16 November 2026. From that date, shareholders will be able to hold and transfer legal title to shares in their own names electronically, without paper share certificates. Existing shareholdings move across in phases over the following five years. This page brings together our announcements, shareholder guidance and key dates for the transition.

What this means for shareholders

Image placeholder 560 × 360

Paper certificates are phased out

Share certificates stop being issued once our shares become participating securities. Certificates you already hold stay valid until your holding is converted, and lost certificates will no longer be replaced.

How dematerialisation works
Image placeholder 560 × 360

Holding shares in your own name

To hold shares electronically in your own name you will need a USI Facility, opened with our Approved Securities Registrar. It lets you view your holding, receive entitlements and transfer shares without paper.

Opening a USI Facility
Image placeholder 560 × 360

Your rights are unchanged

Voting, dividends and the right to attend general meetings are unaffected. What changes is the form your title takes and the way you give instructions to the registrar.
